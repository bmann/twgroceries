Trying Sing Sing which is a Freehouse place that took over the Community Pizza space on The Drive.

Got a steam bun with black vinegar, rosemary fries, shishito peppers, and pho.

Shishito peppers had giant dips and were overly dressed with spices and peanuts … and there were only like 10 peppers? Too expensive for such a small plate.

Probably won’t be back. Expensive for what it is.
